# Landometer in Brief

An interactive new-joiner guide for using the Product Statement—the product's winning strategy—to set the right target and define 100% success for every piece of work.

The core rule is:

> Product Statement = winning strategy. Every work item names the strategic choice it carries, sets the target that helps the product win, and defines complete observable conditions for 100% success.

The **Product Statement is the product’s winning strategy**, not one-to-three-sentence communication copy. It records Who, What, Which and How, lives in the Product Brief, and is cited by exact version/link in every downstream work document. Short audience-specific copy is a derived product description.

“Carried into every document” does not mean pasting the full statement. Each work item needs a Strategy Spine:

- **Strategy ref** — current Product Statement version/link and the strategic choice carried forward.
- **Right target** — what must change for whom, and how that helps the product win.
- **100% success** — the full observable pass conditions, evidence, boundaries, guardrails, owner and review point.

A completed output is `Delivered`. It becomes `Outcome proven` only after the intended user behavior or decision has been tested.

The guide is available in Thai and English and uses one mental model:

- **Who** — the chosen user, their context, decision, and functional and emotional needs.
- **What** — the progress or outcome promised to that user.
- **Which** — current workarounds, direct alternatives, and aspirational benchmarks that set the quality bar.
- **How** — the evidence-backed way to win within that competitive frame.

For a working Product Statement, keep the four-question strategy core to one or two pages. Each answer records a choice, evidence, a test and a boundary. Who goes deep enough to explain switching; Which uses demanding, relevant competitors or benchmarks rather than an easy rival; How names reinforcing choices, comparative evidence and a testable durable-advantage hypothesis—not a feature or technology list. Detailed interviews, comparisons and experiments stay in the Product Brief or evidence appendix.

## Evidence boundary

This public package intentionally excludes the supplied onboarding, GTM, company and methodology PDFs, plus the internal Wiki × Landometer planning file. The location-screening example teaches the method; it is not an approved product claim.

The per-product registry is the only place product-authority links belong. No row is owner-confirmed authoritative yet. The public page contains four links to clean, restricted Drive copies. Those copies are available only to an approved named-viewer allowlist and the account that owns them; they are not public and are not shared to either Workspace domain as a whole. New viewers must be granted access individually. Exposing a link never changes its `candidate`, `historical` or `missing` authority status.
