# Landometer in Brief

An interactive guide to Landometer’s Master Brand Brief, Product Briefs and Product Statements—what each document decides and when to use it.

The guide is available in Thai and English and uses one mental model:

- **Who** — the chosen user, their context, decision, and functional and emotional needs.
- **What** — the progress or outcome promised to that user.
- **Which** — current workarounds, direct alternatives, and aspirational benchmarks that set the quality bar.
- **How** — the evidence-backed way to win within that competitive frame.

## Evidence boundary

This public package intentionally excludes the supplied onboarding, GTM, company and methodology PDFs, plus the internal Wiki × Landometer planning file. The location-screening example teaches the method; it is not an approved product claim.

The current Master Brand Brief has no public canonical link. The Who · What · Which · How framing on this page follows the current brief and the Brand Owner’s clarification of 6 August 2026. `[POSITION-01]` in Design System v0.8.8 still carries the earlier framing and needs a later source update.
